Privacy policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Short version

The 50 Club is an editorial site. We don't sell anything, we don't create accounts, and we don't exploit your personal data. We never sell or rent it to anyone.

What we collect

You don't have to give us anything to read our articles: no name, no email, no phone number. There is no form, no sign-up and no newsletter on this site.

Like any website, our host records technical logs when you visit: IP address, browser type, pages viewed, date and time. These logs are used only for security and to keep the site running, and are kept by the host for a limited period.

Cookies

This site sets no advertising cookies and uses no behavioral tracking tools. That's why you don't see a consent banner: there is nothing to consent to.

The poll

The vote in our articles runs entirely in your browser. Your choice is not sent anywhere, not stored, and not tied to you.

Links to other sites

Our articles link to brand websites. Once you leave The 50 Club, the privacy policy of the site you visit applies, not ours. We encourage you to read it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not maintain a reader database, and we do not sell or share personal information. To make a request or ask a question, write to contact@the50club.co.

Children

This site is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 16.

Changes

We may update this policy if the way the site works changes. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
